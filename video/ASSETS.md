rows, coal plant, roads, power line and terrain are modelled by the build

## locollm-future.mp4

Rendered in Blender from `tools/video/locollm/`. The buildings, rooms,
monitors, laptop, hospital bed, patient monitor, drip stand, office chair,
counter and shelves are modelled by the build script. Everything below was
downloaded by the build and is not stored in the repository. The narration
is the voice track of the earlier version of this film.

### People

Built from MakeHuman data, released as CC0 by the MakeHuman project
(https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks.html). The data
files are read directly; no add-on code is run.

| Asset | Used for | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| MakeHuman base mesh, body-shape targets, default skeleton and weights (from the MPFB 2.0.18 package) | Body, shape and posing | https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mpfb/ | CC0 1.0 (data files) |
| MakeHuman system assets: skins young_asian_female and young_african_male, brown eyes, eyebrow010, eyebrow002, eyelashes01, eyelashes02, hair ponytail01 and short04, shoes01 and shoes03 | Skin, eyes, brows, lashes, hair, shoes | https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html | CC0 1.0 |
| Basic tucked t-shirt and wool pants, by Toigo (shirts01, pants01 packs) | The clinician's scrubs, tinted teal | https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/shirts01.html | CC0 1.0 |
| Male polo shirt, by Namuhekam (shirts01 pack) | The shop owner's shirt | https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/shirts01.html | CC0 1.0 |
