# Third-party assets in the videos

## renewable-transition.mp4

Rendered in Blender from `tools/video/blender/`. The wind turbines, solar
rows, coal plant, roads, power line and terrain are modelled by the build
script. Everything below was downloaded by `tools/video/blender/fetch_assets.py`
and is not stored in the repository.
